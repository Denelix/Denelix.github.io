Dandy's World Calculator v2.1 (Thanksgiving Update)

1. Select a Toon or set stars manually. Toon options appear under the roster.

2. Open Machine Settings for machine types, travel, and accuracy.

3. Open Buffs & Debuffs and hover the purple ? circles for details.

4. Equip up to 2 trinkets. Click a selected trinket to remove it.

5. Open Consumable Items. Click to add 1, or edit the count directly.

6. Hit Calculate! :3

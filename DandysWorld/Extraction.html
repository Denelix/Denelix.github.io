DW Calculator v2.0

1. Select a Toon or set stars manually.

2. Open Machine Settings for machine types, travel, and accuracy.

3. Open Buffs & Debuffs hover the purple ? circles for details.

4. Equip up to 2 trinkets. Click a selected trinket to remove it.

5. Open Consumable Items. click to add 1, or edit the count directly.

6. Hit Calculate! :3

Machines: Stripe (5s SC cooldown) · Circle (3s, 70% base) · Treadmill (speed+stamina, no skillchecks)

Travel: Run to extraction machines, walk to treadmills. Squirm eats books during travel at 50% speed.

Shelly MS: Only your Shelly gets +25% after machines. Teammate Shelly stacks another +25%.
